## How to submit a bubble

1. Open the [online workshop](https://kaitongg-bit.github.io/DIYcodex-bubble/workshop.html), choose **Create a bubble**, and upload a PNG.
2. Tune the stretch guides and text position, and preview short messages, long messages, and narrow/wide layouts.
3. Choose **Publish my bubble**, enter a nickname and bubble name, and confirm sharing rights. The PNG and its current settings enter the review queue together and become public only after approval. Saving or previewing never submits anything, and no account is required.
4. To keep your settings: choose **Save settings** to download the original PNG and `.bubble.json`, then import them into the local studio to continue.

## Review notes

- Review is currently **manual** and takes time; submissions do not go live immediately, so please be patient.
- Once approved, the work appears in the online gallery; if not, the maintainer explains what to fix.
- The gallery stays all-ages. The following are not accepted: nudity or adult content, intimate imagery without consent, sexual content involving minors, hate, harassment, and infringing or illegal content.
- Submitting means you confirm the asset's source and allow the community to display and download it non-commercially; copyright concerns can be reported via a text Issue.
- Download counts come from each PNG's GitHub Releases statistics, may be delayed, and are not unique-user counts; unavailable counts are shown as unavailable.
